About us page

Healing Minds

Healing minds is a non-profitable organization(NPO) that helps and focuses on providing therapy to teenagers or anyone who comes from disadvantaged backgrounds and can’t afford to see and speak to a psychologist. This organizations aims to help and provide support without being judgmental. It is a safe space for any individuals to seek help and guidance for free of charge. This organization’s target audience is troubled teenagers who come from disadvantaged backgrounds and adults who are struggling mentally.

We are here to support anyone facing silent battles. If you ever feel overwhelmed or like you’re sinking, know that you’re not alone — we’re here to help you rise above it.
