Services offered at Healing Minds

• Psychologist - Healing Minds offers a variety pf psychologist both male and female to make sure and individuals are comfortable therefore they can be able to talk freely about the things they are going through.
• Educational resources – Kids we need advice with subject choices, applying for university or need any tutoring
• Sports, arts and culture - Organizing sports, arts and culture activities so that the people in the community can destress and get learn new things that they might find interest in.
• Substance abuse awareness – educating the people in the community about substance abuse the damage it could to their mental and physical, also helping people who are addicts.
• Sex talks – Educating the teenagers about safe sex to avoid teenage pregnancy. This program is very important because teenage pregnancy has become a serious and concerning issues in our communities. This program provides sanitary products and educated young girls about menstruation, helping them feel confident and prepared when they get their periods.
• Food security- Healing minds offers food parcels to anyone in the community who needs food and cannot afford to feed themselves.
