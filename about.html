History

Healing Minds was founded in 2024. Our organization thrives in making sure every individual who needs our help is assisted and they improve their mental health. This Organization has achieved. This organization has impacted a lot of lives, especially for teenagers and young adults who have been through a lot and needed guidance. We are very proud of the significant impact we have made in the community. All the fun activities so that people in the community can destress.

Mission and vision of the organization

Healing Minds organization’s aims to heal and help individuals who are struggling with mental issues and are scared to speak up. The goal of Healing Minds is to help young people, particularly teenagers and young adults to overcome and manage their mental health struggles and navigate life’s challenges with confidence and resilience. Our aspiration is to help more individuals with funding for school and tertiary. We want to be able to guide people without making them feel judged. Our vision is to make people feel a sense of comfort and mental wellness for all.
